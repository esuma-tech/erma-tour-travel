About Ethiopia

A land of ancient history, dramatic landscapes, and living cultures.

Ancient Heritage

Home to the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela, the obelisks of Axum, and the castles of Gondar — testaments to a civilization thousands of years old.

Natural Wonders

From the Simien and Bale mountains to the Danakil Depression and the Great Rift Valley lakes, Ethiopia's landscapes are breathtakingly diverse.

Living Cultures

Over 80 ethnic groups, unique calendars and alphabets, the birthplace of coffee, and some of the world's most vibrant traditions.

Wildlife

Endemic species like the Ethiopian wolf, gelada baboon, and Walia ibex thrive in Ethiopia's protected highlands and forests.

About Erma Tour & Travel

Erma Tour & Travel is an Ethiopia-based tour operator dedicated to sharing the beauty and heritage of our country with travelers from around the world. Based in Addis Ababa, we design authentic, responsible, and memorable journeys — whether you're seeking history, adventure, culture, or nature.
