Admin Overview

Use the buttons above to manage the website:

  • Edit Dashboard: upload/delete gallery images and videos shown publicly.
  • Posts & Packages: create/edit/delete tours (packages, prices, details) and dashboard posts.
  • Bookings: review bookings, update status (pending/confirmed/cancelled/completed), or delete.
  • Inquiries: read and remove contact inquiries.
  • Users: view registered customers and remove accounts if needed.
  • Password Manager: change the admin password.
  • Recommended extras: export reports, manage availability/calendar, testimonials, promotions/coupons, and email notifications.
